1. What the service does

PANKhojo is a private PAN-finding service intended to help a customer locate an existing PAN number that they have forgotten or misplaced. We do not issue new PAN cards, create PAN numbers, amend PAN records or guarantee that a PAN can be found.

2. Eligibility and authority

You must be requesting your own existing PAN information or have lawful authority to act for the person concerned. We may request reasonable information to verify identity, consent or authority. We may decline or pause a request where verification or lawful processing is not possible.

3. Customer information and security

You agree to provide accurate information and to keep your request reference secure. Do not provide Aadhaar OTPs, passwords or account credentials. PANKhojo will not ask you to disclose your Aadhaar OTP. We handle personal information according to the privacy notice presented during the request process.

4. Fees and payment

The applicable fee is displayed before payment. A paid request is an instruction to perform the PAN-finding service; it is not a payment to a government authority and does not purchase or guarantee access to a PAN record. Payment confirmation is sent to the registered email address.

5. Estimated completion time

Our usual PAN-finding time is 2–3 days. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed deadline. Additional verification, incomplete information, provider availability or external record delays may extend the time. We will notify you when the request is completed, and you may track it using your reference number and registered email.

6. Rejection, cancellation and refunds

Requests may be paused, rejected or cancelled if information is inaccurate, identity or authority cannot be verified, the request appears abusive or unauthorised, or the service cannot be lawfully or practically performed. Refund eligibility and timelines are governed by our Cancellation & Refund Policy and applicable law.

7. No guarantee

We cannot guarantee that every existing PAN number can be located. Results depend on the accuracy of the information, verification requirements and availability of lawful retrieval channels. If we cannot provide the service, the refund policy applies.

8. Prohibited use

You must not use the service to obtain another person's PAN without lawful authority, impersonate another person, submit false information, bypass verification or use the service for unlawful purposes.

9. Communications and tracking

We use the email address supplied with the request for transactional updates. Status, customer-facing reasons for rejection/cancellation and milestone dates may be shown on the Track Request page after the request reference and registered email are verified. Private internal notes are not shown to customers.
