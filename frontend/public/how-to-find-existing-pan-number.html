Forgot your PAN number? Safe ways to find your existing PAN

If you have already been allotted a Permanent Account Number (PAN) but cannot remember it, do not apply for another PAN just because you have misplaced the number. First try to retrieve your existing details through records and official channels.

Important: PANKhojo is an independent private PAN-finding service. It is not affiliated with the Income Tax Department, the Government of India, Protean or UTIITSL. We do not issue new PAN numbers or cards. You can use official government channels directly without paying PANKhojo.

1. Check documents and records you already have

  • Look at an old physical PAN card or a downloaded e-PAN PDF, if you have one.
  • Check previous income-tax returns, Form 16 or other tax-related documents where your PAN may be printed.
  • Review bank, investment, demat or insurance records where you previously provided your PAN. Use only accounts and documents you are authorised to access.
  • Search your own email and secure document storage for earlier messages or files from official institutions.

2. Use official Income Tax Department services

Visit the official Income Tax Department e-Filing portal and review its current services and guidance for PAN-related queries. Follow the portal's identity-verification steps. Never share an Aadhaar OTP, password or account login with an agent or an unknown person.

If you suspect that you have been allotted more than one PAN, or your records do not match, contact the relevant official department or authorised PAN service provider for instructions rather than attempting another application.

3. What should you keep ready?

When contacting an official channel or a service provider, be prepared to complete the required identity and authority checks through a lawful, secure process. Only provide information that is necessary. Do not send Aadhaar OTPs, passwords or full identity documents to an unverified contact.

4. How PANKhojo's private service works

  1. Read the service description, eligibility conditions, privacy notice, terms and refund policy before deciding whether to proceed.
  2. Submit a request only for your own existing PAN, or for a person you are legally authorised to represent.
  3. Review the displayed service fee and payment terms before paying.
  4. After payment verification, track the request using the reference number and registered email address.
  5. If the existing PAN is lawfully located and the required checks are completed, PANKhojo will notify you through the registered contact channel.

Our usual processing estimate is 2–3 days, but this is not a guarantee. Some requests may take longer or may not be completable. Any service fee and applicable refund conditions are shown before checkout.

5. Protect your PAN and Aadhaar information

  • Do not share Aadhaar OTPs, UPI PINs, passwords or banking credentials.
  • Do not pay a person who promises guaranteed PAN recovery through unofficial channels.
  • Check the beneficiary name and amount in your UPI app before confirming payment.
  • Keep PAN documents and PDF passwords private.
  • Use official government websites for government services and verify the web address before entering personal information.
