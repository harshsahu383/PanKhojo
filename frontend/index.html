Forgot Your PAN? Find Your Existing PAN

Forgot your PAN number? PANKhojo is an independent private service helping existing PAN holders understand options for locating their PAN. Where required by an authorised process, only an Aadhaar number is requested—not an Aadhaar photo or document.

Important: PANKhojo is an independent private service. It is not affiliated with the Government of India, the Income Tax Department, Protean or UTIITSL. You can use official government services directly.
